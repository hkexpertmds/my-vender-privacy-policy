My Vender is a dairy and milk-vending business management application. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store and protect information when you use the application.

This policy is intended to describe the application's current data practices. Please replace the date placeholders and review the policy with your legal adviser before publication.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information: name, username or login ID, mobile number, email address, password, business name, address, state, city or district, and pincode.
  • Business information: customer and staff records, products, routes, milk orders, quantities, rates, bills, payments, requests and ledger entries.
  • Uploaded content: profile pictures, company logos, payment QR codes and payment screenshots that you choose to upload.
  • Technical information: login session data, authentication tokens, language and theme preferences, the last viewed screen and offline cache data. The app may use IndexedDB, localStorage and sessionStorage in your browser.
  • Address lookup information: during registration, a pincode or district may be sent to a lookup service to help populate address details.

2. How We Use Information

  • To create accounts, verify logins and provide role-based access.
  • To manage milk delivery, customer ledgers, orders, billing, payments, routes and support requests.
  • To provide offline access, data synchronization, backup and restore features, and app preferences.
  • To maintain security, prevent misuse, manage sessions, troubleshoot issues and improve the service.
  • To manage license or subscription status and send necessary service communications.

3. How We Share Information

We do not sell or rent your personal information. Information may be processed by the following parties when necessary to provide and operate the service:

  • Supabase: to store and synchronize account and business records.
  • Hosting and server providers: to run, secure and maintain the application.
  • Third-party services: including pincode lookup, CDN libraries, chart or asset delivery, and payment-related tools when a relevant feature is used.
  • Legal authorities: when disclosure is required by law, court order or a legitimate security requirement.

Customer and staff information added by a business owner may be visible to authorized users of that business according to their assigned role and permissions.

4. Passwords and Security

Passwords are stored on the server in hashed form. After login, the application may store an authentication token and limited session information in your browser. We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards, but no method of internet transmission or electronic storage is completely secure.

Please do not share your password, login ID, one-time password or payment information with unauthorized persons.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for account management, business operations, legal, tax, security or dispute-resolution purposes. You may request account or data deletion using the contact details below. After verification, applicable records may be deleted or anonymized, subject to legal and business retention requirements.

6. Your Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may request access to, correction of, updates to or deletion of your personal information, or ask questions about how it is processed. Please include your registered mobile number or email address and account role so that we can verify your identity.

7. Children's Privacy

This application is intended for business use and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 18.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be published on this page and the "Last updated" date above will be revised.
